Blockchain-Based Digital Identity SystemSelf-sovereign identity on Ethereum. Only hashes go on-chain, never personal data.

1. Contract & account

Paste the address of your deployed DigitalIdentity contract (from Remix or node deploy.js).

Account: not connected

Admin: manage trusted issuers

Only the account that deployed the contract can do this.

User: register my identity

Your details are hashed in your browser. Only the hash is stored on-chain.

Issuer: issue a credential

Attach the document (or type its content). The browser computes its keccak256 hash and only that hash is written to the chain. The user must be registered first.
